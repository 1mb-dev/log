Someone referenced a post from April. It was July.

Three months up. No comments. No shares. The numbers said nothing had happened.

Something had. In April, someone read it. They had no reason to mention it until July.

Most builders check daily. The signal takes months, when it comes at all.

The dashboard doesn't count who read it. It counts who said so.

Most never say so.
